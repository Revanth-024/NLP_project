# Academic Calendar

**Synthetic educational data created for NLP workshop purposes.**

Hindu College of Engineering is a completely fictional educational institution created only for this workshop.

## Academic year and cohort structure

The academic year is 2026-2027. The four-year B.Tech CSE program contains eight semesters, but those semesters are not compressed into one year's calendar. Different cohorts attend different stages concurrently. The odd term serves Semesters 1, 3, 5 and 7; the even term serves Semesters 2, 4, 6 and 8. Machine Learning therefore belongs to the odd-term Semester 5 cohort, NLP to the even-term Semester 6 cohort, and Deep Learning to the odd-term Semester 7 cohort.

Dates below are inclusive and written in ISO form so a date can be distinguished from a course code or semester number. A date range indicates an institutional window, not a separate appointment for every course. These distinctions support grounded answers when a student asks about an individual subject rather than the term as a whole.

## Specific NLP examination

Natural Language Processing (HCE-CSE603), Semester 6, has its end-semester examination on 2027-05-07. This date lies inside the even-term end-semester examination window. It is the only separately fixed course examination date in this contract. The syllabus provides the five units included in the course; the calendar provides the scheduled date, not the syllabus content.

Semester 6 begins on 2027-01-11. Its laboratory examinations run from 2027-04-26 through 2027-04-30. The NLP laboratory belongs to this term, but no individual practical appointment is specified. A response to a lab-date query should therefore report the window and identify the limit of available scheduling detail.

## Interpreting windows and milestones

Internal examinations occur during teaching. Laboratory examinations follow the teaching-end milestone, and written end-semester examinations follow the laboratory window. The semester break begins after end-semester examinations finish. Records and coursework are due at the stated term milestone; the laboratory guidelines explain what belongs in an experiment record. Project-specific milestones name their course codes so they are not mistaken for a deadline applying to every student.

The odd-term break ends before the even term commences. The even-term break is the last listed period of this academic year. The next academic year's commencement is not defined here, and the day after a break must not be presented as a separately approved future commencement date. Reassessment opportunities are announced separately; this calendar does not supply dates for them.

## Using the calendar with other records

An examination date does not by itself establish a student's eligibility. Attendance Policy determines participation eligibility, and Examination Regulations define the passing scheme. A question about both NLP content and its examination needs the NLP Syllabus together with this calendar. A question about another course's individual exam date cannot be answered more precisely than the approved term window.

All date claims in this document are derived from the canonical academic calendar. No weekday, examination duration, room assignment or alternate sitting has been added. Keeping those details unspecified is preferable to inventing a precise but unsupported schedule.
