# Faculty Course Information

**Synthetic educational data created for NLP workshop purposes.**

Hindu College of Engineering is a completely fictional educational institution created only for this workshop.

## Roster scope

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering maintains a small fictional roster for the AI/NLP storyline and selected retrieval-teaching courses. This document gives only the assignments recorded in the canonical contract. Faculty names are invented for the workshop and do not identify real institutional staff. No contact address, office location or personal biography is supplied.

Some courses have no named faculty in the contract. That means the dataset leaves the assignment unspecified, not that a course has no instructor or will not be taught. A reader must not infer a missing assignment from specialization, course similarity or an instructor's role in another subject. The roster is deliberately small so repeated references across syllabi remain easy to inspect.

## Interpreting the AI and language assignments

Dr. Veyana Teral teaches Machine Learning and its laboratory in Semester 5. That pairing helps a reader connect a predictive-learning syllabus with its practical course without combining their separate codes or credit counts. The roster records the two assignments explicitly; it does not authorize assigning this instructor to all courses that discuss prediction.

Dr. Nivora Pellin teaches Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing and the NLP laboratory in Semester 6. The specialization connects language processing with retrieval, but each course retains its own purpose and identifier. A query about who teaches NLP should return the named NLP assignment rather than selecting any faculty member whose specialization sounds related.

Dr. Arveth Solan teaches Deep Learning, Computer Vision and the Deep Learning laboratory in Semester 7. The neural and visual themes create useful comparison opportunities with the Semester 5 and 6 subjects. They do not imply that NLP is a prerequisite for Deep Learning or that its Semester 6 instructor also teaches the neural course.

Ms. Tavira Nolen teaches Artificial Intelligence in Semester 5 and Responsible Computing in Semester 6. Mr. Keshan Velor teaches Computer Networks and its laboratory in Semester 4, and Distributed Systems in Semester 7. These cross-semester assignments are part of the roster, not evidence that those courses run in one semester together.

## Limits and related records

Course Prerequisites determines academic dependencies. Syllabi explain content, and the calendar determines approved dates. A faculty assignment does not create a prerequisite, an exam date or a placement-coordinator appointment. The Placement FAQ names only a contact role; none of the instructors here has been assigned that role.

When course and faculty records overlap, use the stable course code and faculty ID to confirm the relationship. A syllabus can cite its instructor while this document presents the full assigned-course list. Both views must preserve the same relationship without adding unnamed staff or real-world identities.
