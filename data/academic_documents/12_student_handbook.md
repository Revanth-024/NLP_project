# Student Handbook

**Synthetic educational data created for NLP workshop purposes.**

Hindu College of Engineering is a completely fictional educational institution created only for this workshop.


## Welcome to the synthetic academic setting

Hindu College of Engineering is located in the invented town of Velmora within Teralin Learning District. This handbook introduces the fictional B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering program for 2026-2027. Its duration is four years and eight semesters. The setting exists to support educational experiments; it does not describe a real campus, admissions authority or accredited institution.

The handbook is an orientation rather than a replacement for detailed policies. It tells readers which academic record answers a particular kind of question. A short summary may omit detail while remaining consistent with the complete rule. When a question requires a numerical threshold, a precise date or a prerequisite list, consult the relevant detailed document rather than expanding a brief handbook sentence into a guessed answer.

## Understanding the learning pathway

Early semesters develop programming, mathematical reasoning and data organization. Systems and software-development courses provide a broader computing foundation. The later pathway includes Machine Learning in Semester 5, Natural Language Processing in Semester 6 and Deep Learning in Semester 7. These subjects are connected but not interchangeable: learning from examples, working with language and building multilayer neural representations have different emphases.

The complete curriculum has 47 courses and 160 credits. Its listed electives are the fixed choices for this cohort. The semester register supplies course placement, while Course Prerequisites supplies the declared earlier preparation. A laboratory's associated theory subject is a concurrent corequisite, not an extra earlier prerequisite. Keeping this distinction clear helps readers interpret a course relationship accurately.

## Staying informed about participation and assessment

Students must satisfy institutional attendance requirements in each enrolled course. Laboratory and project participation use the general rule, rather than a separate participation standard. Attendance Policy explains the single minimum, shortage consequences and correction of recording errors. A brief statement that a student participates regularly is not enough to decide eligibility without the affected course's record.

Examination Regulations describe the common marking scheme and both numerical pass conditions. Assessment modes differ across theory, laboratories, projects and seminars, but the scheme remains shared. Grades are assigned only after pass conditions are met. Attendance eligibility, marks, grades and credits are related records with distinct meanings, so a result in one should not be used to silently infer another.

## Working in laboratories

Keep the input, method, observations and conclusion connected in an experiment record. The common guidelines require a dated record for each assigned experiment, completion of assigned work before the practical, and a demonstration with explanation during the viva. Incomplete work affects continuous coursework; it does not introduce a separate examination eligibility threshold.

Use approved datasets and software, protect passwords and personal information, treat equipment responsibly, and acknowledge collaboration. A practical result should be explainable by the student who presents it. The common guidelines do not yet define detailed experiment lists or mandate a downloaded model. Those materials belong to later course and workshop activities.

## Reading schedules and preparing for later stages

The academic calendar groups odd and even semesters by concurrent cohorts. It includes teaching periods, assessment windows, record milestones and breaks. The NLP examination has a specific fixed date; other individual course examinations have windows only. A semester break does not establish an unlisted future commencement or reassessment appointment.

Placement preparation is available in Semesters 5 through 8, including students with pending courses. Recruitment registration has separate final-year conditions. Consult Placement FAQ for the difference rather than assuming preparation access guarantees registration or employment. Named faculty assignments appear in Faculty Course Information only where the canonical roster defines them.

## Choosing the right document

Use the curriculum for the overall program, the semester register for placement, syllabi for topic content, prerequisites for dependencies, and faculty information for instructors. Use Attendance Policy for participation, Examination Regulations for evaluation and grading, Laboratory Guidelines for practical work, and the calendar for dates. Questions crossing those boundaries may need evidence from more than one document. No document should invent a missing fact just to make a response look complete.
